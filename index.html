Artist identity

Raymann Rayy's creative practice spans songwriting, composition, music direction and the development of cinematic music concepts. His work explores love, longing, memories, relationships, solitude and human connection, drawing on Indian musical influences and contemporary pop, R&B, romantic, devotional and cinematic forms.

Creative positioning: An artist and storyteller shaping an evolving musical universe where sound, visual language and emotion meet.

The Night Man

The Night Man is an interconnected cinematic musical universe created by Raymann Rayy. It is an extension of his personality, experiences, imagination and artistic expression—not a separate artist identity.

Inspired by the transition from sunset into twilight and moonlight, its creative pillars are Sunset (where emotions awaken), Moon (the inner world finding light) and XOXO (love and connection). Individual songs can stand alone while visuals, themes and recurring details reveal connections between chapters.
